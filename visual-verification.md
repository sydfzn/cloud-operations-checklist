# Visual verification

Desktop and mobile screenshots were captured for `/`, `/analytics`, `/customers`, `/lead-review`, and `/settings` after the multi-customer governance updates.

The overview shows the customer workspace selector, checklist cadence navigation, customer-scoped review copy, progress metrics, and escalation capture. Analytics shows customer selection, date range, cadence filtering, KPI cards, trend, state breakdown, category performance, and cadence comparison. Customer inventory shows the admin boundary and empty-state behavior when no customer records exist. Lead review clearly communicates the approval gate and publication prerequisites. Administration settings shows persisted non-secret Microsoft metadata, directory-user mapping controls, and publication policy.

The mobile layouts remain usable: navigation collapses, forms stack, customer inventory cards remain readable, and the settings mapping form converts to a vertical flow. The current screenshot state is unauthenticated/empty-data, so customer-specific records and populated charts will appear after an administrator creates customer inventory and a lead begins saving checklist progress.
